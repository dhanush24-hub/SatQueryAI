## Hurricane Florence flood evidence
- flood-optical.jpg — 2018-09-19, Landsat 8 OLI, Trent River near Trenton, North Carolina. False-color visible/infrared composite, bands 6-5-4. NOT natural color.
- Optical source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/a-broad-view-of-flooding-in-the-carolinas-92786/
- Optical credit: NASA Earth Observatory / Joshua Stevens; Landsat data from USGS.
- flood-sar-derived.jpg — flood hazard 2018-09-14, North Carolina coast. Authentic Sentinel-1 SAR-derived ARIA flood proxy map: blue highlights likely flooded areas. NOT raw SAR amplitude and NOT model output created by SatQuery.
- SAR source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/florence-inundates-the-carolinas-92775/
- SAR credit: NASA Earth Observatory / Joshua Stevens and Lauren Dauphin; modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2018), processed by ESA and analyzed by NASA-JPL/Caltech ARIA.
- Images have DIFFERENT geographic footprints and dates. Show complementary evidence panels, not an aligned swipe, co-registered pair, or before/after pair. Radar gives regional flood context, optical provides Trent River detail.
